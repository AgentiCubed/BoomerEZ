What we promise

  • Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.
  • Your files stay on your computer. They are never sent to us.
  • Every unusual word is explained on the same page where it appears.
  • You cannot break anything. Nothing happens until you decide it should.
  • Large text, big buttons, and no dragging required.
  • It works without an internet connection.

Why this exists

Most software assumes you already know its vocabulary. When you don't, it treats that as your failure rather than its own. That assumption has quietly locked capable people out of tools they would use well.

These programs make the opposite assumption. You bring the idea, the taste, and the details only you know. The software handles the rest.
